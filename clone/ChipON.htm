About ChipON

ChipON Microelectronics is a Chinese semiconductor company that specializes in 8-bit and 32-bit microcontrollers based on the 8051 and ARM Cortex-M cores. They offer a wide range of MCUs tailored for automotive, industrial, consumer, and IoT applications. ChipON is known for its high-reliability products with enhanced ESD and EMI immunity, making them suitable for harsh environments. Their 8-bit KF8 series is based on the 8051 architecture, while the 32-bit KF32 series uses ARM Cortex-M0, M0+, M3, and M4 cores. ChipON also provides integrated solutions with built-in EEPROM, analog peripherals, and motor control modules. The company has gained traction in the domestic Chinese market and is expanding globally. Many of their devices are used in automotive body electronics, battery management systems, and smart meters. ChipON MCUs often include security features such as flash read protection, write protection, and a unique device ID. They also support encrypted bootloaders to prevent unauthorized firmware updates. Despite being a relatively newer player, ChipON devices are increasingly encountered in legacy and modern systems. Our reverse engineering team has experience with both KF8 and KF32 series. We have developed tools to interface with their programming and debugging ports (SWD, JTAG, or custom ISP). For locked devices, we apply side-channel analysis, fault injection, and invasive decapsulation to recover the firmware. ChipON's security is often less complex than that of Tier-1 vendors, but they still offer useful protection. However, we have successfully bypassed their protections on multiple occasions. We can extract the full flash memory, recover EEPROM data, and even clone the device onto a new chip. Our service is valuable for clients who need to recover lost source code, analyze competitor products, or produce backup replacements. We provide detailed documentation of the recovered firmware and can assist with disassembly and re-engineering. With our expertise, we can decrypt, readback, hack, extract, dump, recover, read, duplicate, and clone ChipON microcontrollers efficiently.

Security Features and Our Attack Approach

ChipON microcontrollers offer several security mechanisms. The primary protection is the flash read-out protection (ROP) that prevents reading the flash memory via the debug interface. Once enabled, the SWD/JTAG access is restricted. ChipON also provides a write protection that blocks erasing or programming certain sectors. Some devices have a bootloader that can be secured with a password or cryptographic challenge. Additionally, ChipON MCUs include a unique 96-bit device ID that can be used for software license verification. However, these protections are not as robust as those from leading vendors. Our reverse engineering team has found that the ROP can often be bypassed by simply using a timing attack or voltage glitch during the boot process. By corrupting the read of the lock bits, we can trick the device into granting full access. We have also used the SWD interface with special sequences to unlock the device. For the 8-bit KF8 series, we have developed a custom programmer that can override the protection using a reset glitch. For the 32-bit KF32, we have employed side-channel power analysis to extract the password used for the bootloader. In addition, we have decapsulated devices to directly read the flash memory using a microscope and probe the floating-gate transistors. This is a reliable but invasive method. We also offer cloning services, where we take a locked device, extract its firmware, and program a blank ChipON MCU with the same binary. This is useful for replacing obsolete parts. Our lab has the necessary equipment to handle both 8-bit and 32-bit ChipON devices. We have a deep understanding of their architecture and programming protocols. We also maintain a library of known vulnerabilities and exploits for various ChipON families. Our success rate is very high, and we can usually deliver the extracted firmware within a few days.
