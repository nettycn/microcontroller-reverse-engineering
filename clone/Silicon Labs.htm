About Silicon Labs

Silicon Labs is a leading provider of silicon, software, and solutions for IoT, industrial, and consumer markets. Their microcontroller portfolio includes the 8‑bit EFM8 (based on 8051) and the 32‑bit EFM32 (ARM Cortex‑M). They are known for ultra‑low‑power devices, making them ideal for battery‑powered applications. Silicon Labs also produces wireless SoCs combining MCU with Bluetooth, Zigbee, and Thread radios. Their products are widely used in smart home, medical, and industrial sensing. Silicon Labs emphasizes ease of development with the Simplicity Studio IDE. They offer a comprehensive SDK with support for multiple protocols. Their 8‑bit EFM8 series is a modern take on the classic 8051 architecture. The EFM32 Gecko family is famous for its energy‑efficient operation. Silicon Labs also provides security solutions including Secure Vault technology. This hardware‑based security protects against physical and remote attacks. They have a strong focus on connectivity and wireless integration. Silicon Labs’ MCUs often include advanced analog peripherals and capacitive sensing. They are a key supplier for many consumer IoT devices. Silicon Labs’ products are automotive‑grade for some series. They provide extensive application notes and reference designs. The company has a strong commitment to quality and reliability. Silicon Labs’ MCUs are used in smart meters, lighting controls, and wearables. They offer a wide range of memory and package options. Their devices support multiple power modes for efficient energy management. Silicon Labs also produces clock generators and isolators. They have a global sales and support network. The company is at the forefront of edge computing with AI‑enabled MCUs. Their security features include tamper detection and cryptographic acceleration. Silicon Labs’ roadmap includes support for Matter and other emerging standards. They are a trusted partner for many Fortune 500 companies.

Silicon Labs Security Features

Silicon Labs integrates advanced security into its MCUs, especially the Secure Vault technology. This includes a hardware root of trust, secure key management, and anti‑tamper protection. The devices feature a unique serial number and secure boot. Flash memory can be locked to prevent readback. Debug interfaces can be disabled with a password or OTP fuses. However, attackers may attempt to hack the device using fault injection. Side‑channel power analysis can reveal keys during encryption. Our team has successfully extracted firmware from EFM8 devices using glitch attacks. We have also used electromagnetic analysis to recover keys from EFM32. Invasive decapsulation allows direct probing of the flash array. Silicon Labs’ security is robust, but older devices have known vulnerabilities. We can bypass the lock bits by manipulating the voltage supply. We also use FIB to cut security fuses. Our lab can recover the entire firmware even when read protection is enabled. We also offer to clone the device for legacy replacement. Silicon Labs’ ongoing security improvements mean we must constantly evolve. MikaTech provides comprehensive extraction and analysis services.
