Holtek Semiconductor Reverse Engineering

Comprehensive services for Holtek 8-bit MCUs and touch keys

Brand Overview

Holtek Semiconductor is a Taiwanese company specialising in 8‑bit microcontrollers, widely used in consumer electronics, home appliances, industrial controls, and LED drivers. Their microcontrollers are based on a proprietary RISC architecture, offering both OTP and flash‑based versions for production flexibility. Holtek also produces dedicated touch key controllers and LED drivers, and many legacy products from the 1990s and 2000s use Holtek chips.

We have extensive experience in reverse engineering Holtek devices, extracting firmware from both flash and OTP variants. Holtek’s security features include a code protection bit that prevents readback, but we have developed effective methods to bypass this protection. We can also recover data from Holtek’s EEPROM and RAM, and our services are used by companies needing to clone old Holtek controllers. We provide full firmware extraction, disassembly, and analysis, identifying all functions and data structures to help clients understand the operation of their devices.

We also offer support for migrating Holtek code to modern MCUs, and our success rate on Holtek projects is nearly 100%, with over 150 projects completed. Our expertise covers all Holtek families, including HT46, HT48, HT66, HT68, and touch controllers like the HT45 series. We can recover calibration data and touch sensitivity parameters to ensure clones perform exactly like the original. We provide confidential service with strict NDAs and are available for quick turnarounds or complex analyses, even assisting in legal disputes with expert testimony.

Security Features & Reverse Engineering Insights

Holtek microcontrollers incorporate a code protection fuse that, when enabled, prevents reading the program memory. This fuse is blown during programming and cannot be reset under normal conditions. However, we have discovered that by manipulating the clock signal during power‑up, we can bypass this protection – this glitch causes the chip to ignore the fuse and allow readback, enabling us to dump the entire flash or OTP memory. For OTP devices, there is no way to reprogram, but we can extract the data using advanced micro‑probing to read each bit from the die, a time‑consuming but reliable method that has successfully recovered code from Holtek OTP chips considered lost.

For flash‑based devices, we can also use the ISP (In‑System Programming) interface; even when ISP is disabled, we can reactivate it by a voltage glitch, allowing us to read the memory without opening the package. We can also extract EEPROM data used for calibration and configuration. Holtek's touch controllers have their own set of security bits, which we can bypass using similar techniques. Our side‑channel analysis can recover encryption keys if the device uses them, and we have reverse‑engineered proprietary scrambling algorithms to decrypt data and clone the device perfectly, including duplicating the unique ID if it exists.

Our team has developed a library of known vulnerabilities for Holtek, allowing us to often complete the extraction in less than a week. We provide a detailed report of the extraction process and results, and we offer a disassembly service to convert machine code to assembly with annotations for easier understanding. Our clients have used our services to produce second‑source devices and in litigation cases regarding IP theft. We stay current with Holtek’s new releases and security updates, and we have already mastered the latest HT66 series. Our pricing is competitive with discounts for bulk cloning, and all projects are handled with absolute confidentiality. Let us help you unlock the potential of your Holtek devices.
