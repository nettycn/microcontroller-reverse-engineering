PCB Clone Services

Precision replication of printed circuit boards – from simple 2‑layer to complex HDI designs

What We Offer

Our PCB reverse engineering service goes beyond simple copying. We deliver complete documentation packages including schematics, bill of materials, Gerber files, and netlists – all verified through rigorous testing.

Multi‑Layer Extraction

Using advanced imaging and delamination techniques, we accurately reconstruct internal traces and vias for boards with 4, 6, 8, or more layers. Blind and buried vias are no obstacle for our process.

Schematic Capture

We convert physical board routing into logical schematics, with component values, reference designators, and net names. This enables you to understand the circuit functionality, modify designs, or create a replacement board.

Component Identification

We identify and cross‑reference all components, including custom ICs, passives, and connectors. For obsolete parts, we suggest compatible replacements or design‑in alternatives.

Quality Assurance

Every clone is verified against the original using in‑circuit testing and functional validation. We guarantee that our deliverables are electrically and mechanically identical to the source board.

Whether you need to reproduce a legacy control board, reverse‑engineer a competitor’s product, or create a drop‑in replacement for end‑of‑life hardware – MikaTech delivers with speed and accuracy.
