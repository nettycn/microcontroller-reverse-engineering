What we do

Core Reverse Engineering Services

From read-out protection bypass to full system reconstruction — we deliver deep technical insights for embedded systems.

Security Bypass

Bypass read-out protection (RDP), JTAG/SWD locks, and other security mechanisms on a wide range of MCUs.

Firmware Extraction

Dump internal flash, EEPROM, and OTP memory using glitching, side-channel, or invasive techniques.

Binary Analysis

Disassemble, decompile, and analyze machine code to understand functionality, algorithms, and vulnerabilities.

Silicon Reverse

Delayering, imaging, and netlist extraction for hardware-level understanding and clone detection.

Security Audits

Comprehensive security evaluation of embedded devices, including fault injection and side-channel resistance.

Legacy Revival

Recover and reconstruct firmware for obsolete or undocumented microcontrollers to extend product life.

MCU Decapsulation

Precision removal of epoxy or ceramic packaging to expose the silicon die for visual inspection, failure analysis, and direct probing of internal nodes.

Chip Identification

Identify unknown, unmarked, or custom-labeled chips via die markings, pinout analysis, electrical characterization, and cross-referencing with known databases.

Hardware Reproduction

PCB Clone & Reverse Engineering

Reconstruct, replicate, and optimize printed circuit boards from physical samples — with full schematic capture and BOM generation.

PCB Reverse Engineering

Extract netlist, component placement, and layer stack-up from bare boards or assembled PCBs with high precision.

Schematic Recovery

Rebuild full circuit schematics from physical boards, including power trees, signal routing, and critical buses.

BOM Generation

Identify and list all components with part numbers, manufacturers, and alternatives for supply chain continuity.

PCB Reproduction

Generate production-ready Gerber files, drill data, and assembly drawings for exact or improved board copies.
