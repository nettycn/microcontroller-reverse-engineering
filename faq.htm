Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our reverse engineering services

What is the typical turnaround time for a project?

Depending on complexity, most MCU extraction projects take between 2 to 6 weeks. PCB cloning with full schematic recovery may take 3 to 8 weeks. We provide detailed time estimates during the initial consultation.

Is my data kept confidential?

Absolutely. We sign a non‑disclosure agreement (NDA) with every client. All project files are stored on encrypted, access‑controlled servers, and physical samples are handled in secure lab areas with strict chain‑of‑custody procedures.

Do you support custom or obscure MCU architectures?

Yes. Our team has experience with over 50 different architectures. If it’s not in our dropdown list, contact us – we likely have the tools and knowledge to handle it.

Can you reverse‑engineer a device without damaging it?

For many devices, we offer non‑invasive side‑channel methods. However, some heavily secured chips require decapsulation or FIB modifications, which may be destructive. We always discuss the best approach with you before starting.

What deliverables do you provide?

For MCU projects: extracted firmware (binary and hex), disassembly listings, and optionally decompiled C code. For PCB projects: schematics, Gerbers, BOM, netlist, and 3D models. All deliverables are provided in industry‑standard formats.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is project‑dependent based on device complexity, security level, and required output. We offer a free preliminary assessment and fixed‑price quotes – no hidden fees.
