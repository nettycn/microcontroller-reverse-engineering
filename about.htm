About MikaTech

Excellence in silicon‑level reverse engineering since 2010

Who We Are

MikaTech is a global leader in microcontroller reverse engineering, PCB cloning, and secure firmware extraction. Our team of hardware security experts, embedded engineers, and cryptanalysts work together to solve the most complex challenges in legacy system recovery, IP verification, and competitive analysis.

Our Mission

We bridge the gap between obsolete hardware and modern innovation. By recovering critical design data from locked or discontinued microcontrollers, we empower our clients to extend product lifecycles, re-engineer legacy systems, and protect their intellectual property.

Why Choose MikaTech?

  • 10+ years of hands‑on experience with 8‑bit to 32‑bit architectures.
  • State‑of‑the‑art lab – FIB, SEM, laser ablation, side‑channel analysis, and fault injection.
  • Strict confidentiality – all projects are NDA‑protected and handled with utmost discretion.
  • Global reach – serving automotive, aerospace, medical, and industrial clients across 30+ countries.

Our Team

Our core engineers hold advanced degrees in microelectronics and computer science, with decades of combined experience at leading semiconductor firms. We continuously invest in research to stay ahead of emerging security countermeasures.
